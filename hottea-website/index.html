Privacy & Security

Clear Privacy Principles

Privacy is an architectural requirement, not marketing rhetoric. Here is exactly what HotTea does and does not do.

Zero First-Party Telemetry

HotTea contains zero tracking scripts, usage telemetry, ad networks, or background analytics beacons. Your browsing patterns remain on your device.

Local-First Offline Storage

Bookmarks, notes, history, and workspace configurations are written to a local SQLite database (`$HOME/Documents` or sandbox container).

Explicit Network Transparency

Search queries are sent directly to your chosen provider (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave). We do not route search traffic through proprietary proxy servers.

Important Real-World Disclaimer

HotTea does not make you anonymous on the internet. External websites and search providers still observe your public IP address, TLS connection metadata, and search queries as they do in any web browser. For anonymous network routing, consider dedicated anonymity tools such as the Tor Browser.
